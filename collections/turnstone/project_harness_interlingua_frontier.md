**V\* = the interlingua, typed correctly.** "V is the interlingua" is a category error as stated (`V*: S→ℝ` is a scalar potential; an interlingua is a *space*). The correct reading is a duality: a cost-to-go induces a representation (its level sets coordinatize meaning) and vice-versa. **Koopman** — lift the nonlinear system into the right observables → dynamics linearize, eigenfunctions = natural coords, Lyapunov fns fall out of them. **RL** — the successor representation is the resolvent `(I−γP)⁻¹`, and `V = SR·r` is a *linear readout* of it (value-equivalent models). So the right intermediate representation and the progress-certificate are **two faces of one object** — the interlingua is the change of coordinates in which V\* stops being model-complex. Explains the formalism's central limit: V\* doesn't compress *in raw token coordinates*; in the right representation it might. We just don't have the representation.

**The two orthogonal walls.** (1) `|W|` — the **substrate / dictionary**, fixed at training: what's representable at all; the source of V\*-incompressibility. NOT bounded by context. (2) `L` — the **working-memory tape** (the L×d residual stream): a single forward pass is constant-depth (`TC⁰`), so sequential depth is bought only by emitting tokens → context = tape, autoregressive loop = read/write head, inner solver ≈ a **linear-bounded automaton**, reachable fixpoints capped by space-`O(L)`. CoT = register-spilling onto the tape (`L` as RAM). Adds a 2nd non-haltable region `U(L)` ("doesn't fit", V\* undefined) beside `D` ("runs forever", V\*=∞): `dom(V*) ⊆ reachable(L) \ D`. `|W|` and `L` are **substitutable on one budget line** (pretraining-scaling ↔ inference-scaling — train amortizes compute into W, inference spends it on L). Inherent to *finite working memory*, not attention — SSMs (Mamba) make it tighter (fixed register set); lost-in-the-middle ⇒ usable tape < L.

**The plant as a band-limited wave-render of a fractal.** Human language is **scale-free** (Zipf = power-law = self-similarity). A finite model holds a **band-limited** projection, packing >d features in **superposition** (interfering near-orthogonal directions; softmax = |ψ|² over V). **Neural scaling laws = the fractal's toll** — power-law loss = each octave of zoom costs exponentially more; *the same fact* as the Zipf structure, not adjacent to it (can't scale to the bottom of a fractal). The render is **low-pass, not a crop** (something everywhere, sharpness ∝ data mass) — which is *why* it both generalizes AND **confabulates** (interpolating smooth across a sub-resolution spike → rounds to the nearest representable point on a continuous manifold). Resolution is allocated by the **training measure** → sharp over the crawlable archive, starved over what humanity under-recorded. **Bias = where the bandwidth went; the bandwidth went where the writing was; the writing was never a neutral sample of meaning. The model's blur is the archive's blur** — a self-portrait of the record.

The only representation of either is `W` (a measure of everything sayable, not a grammar for saying it). **Finite-scale shadow already filed: #699** — even across 2–3 provider wire formats the canonical neutral block dialect can't be written cleanly (the `native` lane leaks the residue); "no interlingua across all human languages" is that same phenomenon at the limit.
